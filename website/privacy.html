Privacy policy

What OneClick OS and this website do and do not collect. Short version: we do not collect personal information, and the system has no tracking.

Last updated 14 November 2026. This document is a draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Who we are

This policy is from [legal name of the publisher to be added], the publisher of OneClick OS, based in New South Wales, Australia. Contact: [contact email to be added]. We aim to follow the Australian Privacy Principles.

2. The operating system

We collect nothing from your computer. OneClick OS has no account, no sign-in to us, no advertising identifier, no usage statistics and no crash reports sent to us. The usage-feedback feature of the desktop it is built on has been removed, and its setting is fixed to off.

Some features have to contact other services to work. When they do, those services see your internet address (IP address), as any website does:

  • Checking for and downloading updates contacts GitHub's container registry (ghcr.io), which stores our system versions. GitHub's privacy statement applies to those requests. We do not receive a list of who downloaded what.
  • The App Store contacts Flathub to list and download apps. Flathub's privacy policy applies.
  • Setting the clock and checking for an internet connection contact standard time and connectivity servers run by the Fedora Project.
  • Apps you install, such as a web browser, a game launcher or an AI assistant, have their own privacy policies and may collect information. We do not control them.

3. This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts.
  • Fonts, images and scripts are served from this site itself, not from third parties.
  • The apps you tick on the Apps page stay in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • The company that hosts this website keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use them to identify you.
  • The Download buttons take you to a file host. That host sees your IP address when you download.

4. If you contact us

If you email us or file a bug report, we receive what you choose to send, for example your email address and a description of the problem. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem. Bug reports filed on our public GitHub page are visible to everyone, so do not include personal information in them.

5. Children

The website and software do not ask for age or any personal details. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Sharing and overseas storage

We do not sell or share personal information. The services named in section 2 and our website host may store their own logs outside Australia, including in the United States.

7. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or make a complaint by contacting [contact email to be added]. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the system or website starts collecting anything, this policy will say so before it does, and it will be optional.

OneClick OS is free and open source. It is an early test version and not finished.
